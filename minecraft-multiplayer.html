MORE THAN A GAME.

Multiplayer is about more than simply being online at the same time. It is about talking, exploring, building, competing and enjoying the game together.

With AlienZord, you can communicate with other players through Discord Voice Channels or, when supported by the server setup, use in-game voice chat to talk while playing.

Whether you are working together on a large build, exploring the world, playing PvP or simply hanging out with friends, voice communication makes the experience more connected and enjoyable.
