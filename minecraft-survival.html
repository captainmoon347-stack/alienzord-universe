SURVIVAL MODE

Enter a world where every block has a purpose, every journey creates a story and every decision shapes your adventure. Minecraft Survival gives you the freedom to gather, build, explore and create your own legacy inside the AlienZord Universe.

01 — THE EXPERIENCE

BUILD YOUR LEGACY.

Survival begins with almost nothing. Gather your first resources, craft basic tools, find a safe location and create a place that you can call home.

As you progress, your small beginning can become a powerful base, a massive kingdom or a complete world created around your own imagination.

Explore the environment, discover rare resources, improve your equipment and prepare yourself for everything the world can throw at you.

02 — SURVIVAL WORLD

YOUR WORLD. YOUR STORY.

01. Explore

Travel through different biomes, discover villages, caves and structures and search for locations that can become part of your adventure.

02. Gather Resources

Collect wood, stone, ores, food and other materials. Every resource helps you progress further and create better equipment.

03. Build

Create your own home, farms, storage systems, castles, cities or anything else you can imagine.

04. Survive

Prepare yourself for hostile mobs, dangerous environments and unexpected situations. Upgrade your equipment and keep improving your survival skills.

05. Create Your Adventure

Minecraft does not force you to follow one path. Create your own goals, build your own projects and decide what your survival journey means to you.

03 — ALIENZORD COMMUNITY

SURVIVE TOGETHER.

The AlienZord Universe is built around community, creativity and gaming. Connect with other players, share your builds, participate in Minecraft activities and stay updated about upcoming events through our Discord community.

Whether you are a builder, explorer, fighter or someone who simply enjoys creating a world of your own, there is always something new to experience.
