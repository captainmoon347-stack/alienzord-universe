01 — THE EXPERIENCE

SURVIVAL WORLD.

Minecraft Survival is more than simply collecting resources and staying alive. It is about creating something that belongs to you. Start with nothing, gather your first resources, establish your base and slowly turn your world into something remarkable.

02 — SURVIVAL

BUILD YOUR LEGACY.

Survival begins with a simple idea: start with nothing and build something that becomes yours. Gather wood, stone, food and other resources. Craft the tools you need and find a safe place to begin your journey.

As your world grows, your small shelter can become a powerful base. Explore caves, discover new biomes, collect valuable resources and improve your equipment.

There is no single way to play Survival. You can build, explore, farm, trade, fight, travel or simply create your own story. The world is yours to shape.

03 — FIRST STEP

Gather Your First Resources

Begin by collecting basic materials around you. Wood is one of the most important starting resources because it allows you to create basic tools and crafting equipment. Collect enough food to keep yourself prepared and gather stone before travelling too far from your starting area.

04 — BUILD

Establish Your Base

Find a location that feels right for your survival journey. Build a shelter where you can store resources, craft equipment and prepare for your next adventure. Your first base does not need to be perfect. Start small and upgrade it as your world develops.

05 — EXPLORE

Explore & Upgrade

Once your base is secure, start exploring. Discover caves, villages, forests, mountains and other hidden locations. Collect better resources, upgrade your tools and equipment and gradually prepare yourself for more challenging areas of the world.

06 — COMMUNITY

Join the AlienZord Community

Minecraft becomes even better when you share the experience with other players. Join the AlienZord community, meet other players, participate in activities and become part of the growing Minecraft GameHub.

07 — YOUR JOURNEY

START SMART.

Gather your resources. Build your base. Explore the world. Create your own story. Your survival journey starts here.
